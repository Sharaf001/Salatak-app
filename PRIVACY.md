# Salatak Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 18, 2026**

Salatak Prayer Times ("Salatak", "we", or "us") is an Islamic companion app
for prayer times, Quran reading, duas, azkar, qibla direction, Hijri dates,
and personal worship tools. This policy explains what information the app
uses and how it is handled.

## Information the app uses

- **Location:** Salatak may request foreground location access to calculate
  prayer times and the qibla direction. Location is used on your device while
  the relevant feature is active. Salatak does not sell or share your
  location.
- **Notifications:** If you enable prayer notifications, the app uses the
  notification permission to schedule reminders on your device.
- **Photos and media:** If you choose to save a wallpaper, the app requests
  permission to save that selected image to your device's photo library.
- **Local app data:** Bookmarks, the rak'ah counter, and notification
  preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to
  Salatak servers.

Salatak does not require an account and does not collect names, email
addresses, contacts, precise location history, advertising identifiers, or
payment information. We do not use personal data for advertising, and we do
not sell personal data.

## Third-party services

The app may rely on platform services provided by Apple, Google, Expo, or
your device manufacturer to deliver operating-system features such as
location, notifications, photo-library access, and application distribution.
Those services process data under their own privacy policies.

## Children's privacy

Salatak is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The
updated policy will be published at this URL with a new effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the
